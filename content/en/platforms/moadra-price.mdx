## What the Moadra Price Actually Buys You

Moadra is a 2D action-adventure built around exploration, difficult combat, and ability-gated progression. You play as the titular Moadra on Protea VI, a sand-swept alien world where your ancestors went extinct 20,000 years ago. The pitch is grim, atmospheric, and unapologetically hostile — the store tags read like a checklist for genre fans: Metroidvania, sci-fi, horror, exploration, gore, nonlinear, difficult.

The loop is familiar but well-defined. You fight through hostile organisms, absorb Protoplasm from destroyed creatures, and consume it in organic chambers to trigger metamorphosis — permanently evolving your abilities and weapons. A scanner bot with X-ray vision reveals hidden paths and maps the world around you. Movement is fast and deliberate: roll, dodge, wall jump, and sprint at high speed to survive everything from small threats to gargantuan bosses.
