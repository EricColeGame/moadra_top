## What Kind of Game Is Moadra?

Moadra is an exploration-driven action-adventure built around a massive, interconnected world. You play as the title character, a survivor piecing together what happened to the Neera — a civilization that went extinct 20,000 years before the story begins. The structure rewards curiosity: hidden paths, secret rooms, and optional discoveries are the main course, not side dressing.

Combat is deliberately challenging. Gloomsoft describes encounters that scale from minor organisms to gargantuan monsters that force you to use every tool in your kit. That combination — punishing fights plus open-ended exploration — puts Moadra in the same conversation as modern metroidvanias and soulslike-adjacent action games, though its sand-blasted science-fiction identity is very much its own.

The world itself is split across distinct biomes: the desolate surface, deadly freezing summits, and a research facility overrun by hostile alien species. Each area has its own hazards, and the game expects you to adapt rather than brute-force your way through.

## The Systems That Drive Progression

Moadra's growth loop is not a simple XP bar. The game layers traversal upgrades, gear, and biological evolution on top of each other.

| System | How It Works | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Agility moveset | Roll, dodge, leap, wall jump, and sprint at high speed | Movement is your primary defense against organisms and environmental hazards |
| Scanner bot | Deploy it to examine creatures and terrain | Reveals hidden paths and secret areas invisible to the naked eye |
| X-ray vision | Deep-scans your surroundings | Helps map out areas you have already explored |
| Protoplasm | Absorbed from destroyed hostile organisms | The core resource that fuels everything else |
| Organic chambers | Consume Protoplasm inside them | Triggers metamorphosis |
| Metamorphosis | Applies permanent upgrades | Evolves abilities and weapons to new levels |

The elegant part is how these interlock. Exploration finds you resources; resources make you stronger; strength opens previously lethal routes; new routes reveal more of the map. The scanner bot is the quiet MVP here — players who ignore it will find Protea VI far more hostile than it needs to be.

## Getting Started: Practical Tips for Protea VI

The opening hours of Moadra are unforgiving if you rush. These habits will save you a lot of frustration.

| Priority | Action | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scan every new room before fighting | Hidden paths and resource caches are easy to walk past |
| 2 | Practice wall jumps in safe areas | Traversal precision is required, not optional |
| 3 | Bank Protoplasm regularly | Metamorphosis is your main power spike |
| 4 | Note locked routes | You will return with new abilities |
| 5 | Fight one tier below your comfort level | Learning enemy patterns beats trading damage |

A few more pointers:

- **Look up constantly.** Vertical exploration is where most secrets hide in this kind of interconnected world.
- **Treat the freezing summits as a gear check.** If an area is draining you faster than you can react, you are probably there early.
- **Use the research facility for practice.** Dense enemy populations are ideal for learning dodge timing.
- **Do not hoard metamorphosis charges.** Upgrading early compounds across the rest of your run.
- **Keep a mental map of dead ends.** Backtracking is a feature of the genre, and the scanner helps you remember what you skipped.
