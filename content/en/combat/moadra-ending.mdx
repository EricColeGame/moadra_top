## What the Game Tells You About the Final Descent

Gloomsoft's own framing is unusually direct. The studio describes *Moadra* as a story about seeking redemption and confronting "the terrifying sin buried in the depths of the forgotten planet Protea VI." That phrasing is a roadmap. The surface is desolate, the frozen summits are deadly, and the research facility is infested — but the depths are where the game's narrative weight sits.

Practically, that suggests the ending sequence lives below everything you've explored so far, gated behind the traversal abilities you pick up along the way. Two systems matter most in the run-up:

- **Your scanner bot.** X-ray vision reveals hidden paths and secret areas that are invisible to the naked eye. If a wall looks suspicious near the endgame, scan it.
- **Protoplasm and organic chambers.** Blowing up hostile organisms and absorbing their biomass is how you evolve weapons and abilities. Skipping this is the single most common way players arrive at the finale underpowered.

If you're unsure whether you're near the end, ask yourself one question: have you mapped the depths? If the answer is no, keep exploring.

### Don't Hoard Your Metamorphosis

One trap that player experience consistently flags in games like this: saving upgrades "for the boss." Organic chambers are the only place to apply metamorphosis, and if the final area doesn't have one, you've locked yourself out of power you already earned. Spend before you descend. If you find a chamber mid-run, use it immediately.

## Combat Tactics for the Moadra Ending Sequence

The finale compresses *Moadra's* combat into short, dense encounters. You won't have room to reset, so build habits that work in a closet-sized arena.

| Enemy Type | What It Punishes | Counter |
|---|---|---|
| Fast swarming organisms | Standing still to trade hits | Sprint repositioning, then burst damage |
| Gargantuan monsters | Greedy combo extensions | Roll through, punish the recovery window |
| Ranged/hazard hybrids | Ignoring the environment | Use X-ray vision to read the room before engaging |
| Armored elites | Chip damage from a single weapon | Rotate your arsenal and abilities |
| Arena gatekeepers | Panic dodging | Learn one pattern at a time, then add damage |

Three habits separate clean runs from messy ones:

1. **Roll for position, not panic.** Dodging is a movement tool first and an escape second. Roll *through* attacks to end up where you want to be.
2. **Wall jumps are free height.** Vertical arenas are common in the depths. Use them to break line of sight and reset enemy aggression.
3. **Absorb as you go.** Protoplasm drops from the enemies you kill in the final stretch. If the game lets you convert that mid-area, do it — the finale is not the place to leave resources on the floor.

If you're struggling with a specific encounter, drop the difficulty of your approach rather than the game's: slow down, watch one attack cycle, then commit. Most deaths in the ending stretch come from impatience, not from a lack of damage output.
