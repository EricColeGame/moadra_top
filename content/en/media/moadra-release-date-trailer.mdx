| System | How it works | Why it matters in play |

## Practical Tips for Your First Hours on Protea VI

- **Scan early and often.** Hidden paths do not announce themselves. Sweep every new room with the scanner bot before moving on, and treat unusual geometry as a signal that something is concealed.
- **Harvest before you move.** Absorb Protoplasm from creatures while you are already in the area. Backtracking for biomass is far more tedious than clearing a room on the first pass.
- **Bank mutations deliberately.** Organic chambers are limited, so decide which abilities and weapons you want to push first rather than spreading upgrades thin.
- **Learn the movement chain.** Rolling into a wall jump into a sprint covers ground quickly and dodges most hazards. Practice it in safe rooms before relying on it in combat.
- **Respect the verticality.** Frozen summits sit above the surface, and the hazards there behave differently. Stock up before climbing rather than treating it as a quick detour.
