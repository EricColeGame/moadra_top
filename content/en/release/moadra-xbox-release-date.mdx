## What "Release Window" Usually Means in Practice

A launch window is a scheduling intention, and windows move for predictable reasons. Certification on Xbox requires a build that passes platform requirements, which means a game can be content-complete and still not have a date. Marketing timing matters too: publishers rarely drop a date without a trailer, a store page, and pre-orders ready to go live at the same moment.

That's why the Moadra Xbox release date will almost certainly arrive as part of an announcement package rather than as a quiet blog line. Watch for a new trailer with a date card, a Microsoft Store page appearing with a pre-order button, or an Xbox Wire post.
