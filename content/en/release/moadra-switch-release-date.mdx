## What Switch Players Would Be Getting

Moadra drops you on Protea VI, a sand-swept alien world littered with hostile creatures, environmental hazards, and the ruins of a civilization that vanished 20,000 years ago. You play as the titular Moadra, hunting answers about your lost Neera heritage while uncovering what the store description calls a terrifying sin buried deep in the planet.

The moment-to-moment loop leans on agility and information. You roll, dodge, wall jump, and sprint through combat encounters, then switch to a scanner bot with X-ray vision to expose hidden routes and map unexplored territory. Destroyed organisms drop Protoplasm, which you spend in organic chambers to trigger metamorphosis and upgrade your abilities and weapons.

It is a loop built for handheld play: short exploration bursts, permanent progression, and secrets that reward backtracking.
