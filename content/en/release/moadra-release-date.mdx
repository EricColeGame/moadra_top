## What the Launch Version Actually Delivers

Moadra casts you as the titular character on Protea VI, a sand-swept alien world littered with hostile organisms, environmental hazards, and the ruins of a civilization that went extinct 20,000 years ago. Your stated goal is to uncover your lost Neera heritage — and, per the store description, confront a "terrifying sin" buried deep in the planet.

| System | How It Works |
| --- | --- |
| Traversal | Roll, dodge, leap, wall jump, and sprint at high speed |
| Combat | Reflex-driven fights with an expanding weapon arsenal and special abilities |
| Scanner bot | X-ray vision reveals creatures, hidden paths, and secret areas; maps explored zones |
| Protoplasm | Absorbed from destroyed organisms, spent inside organic chambers |
| Metamorphosis | Permanent upgrades to abilities and weapons |

The progression loop is the hook here: you don't just find new gear, you biologically evolve. That distinction matters in a genre where ability-gating is the whole architecture. Community reports from early players suggest the map is genuinely nonlinear, with secrets tucked behind scanner-only sightlines rather than obvious signposting.

## Difficulty, Tone, and Who This Is For

Moadra is tagged as difficult, dark, atmospheric, and gory — and the developers describe it as containing frequent violence and gore. If you're looking for a relaxed exploration game, this isn't it. A few things to expect going in:

- **Punishing but readable combat** — enemy tells matter, and the dodge toolkit is generous once you learn it.
- **Nonlinear routing** — the world is interconnected, and the scanner bot rewards curiosity with shortcuts.
- **Boss-scale threats** — the store page explicitly warns that some encounters force you to use every tool available.
- **Controller-friendly design** — Xbox, DualShock (USB), and DualSense controllers are supported.
