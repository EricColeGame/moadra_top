## What Makes Moadra Different From Other Metroidvanias

Plenty of games promise a "hostile alien world." Moadra's differentiator is that its two headline systems — movement and metamorphosis — feed each other. You are not just unlocking doors with keys; you are changing what your body can do.

### Movement Is the First Weapon

Gloomsoft leans hard into agility. Moadra can roll, dodge, leap, wall jump, and sprint, and the game explicitly frames speed as a survival tool rather than a combat flourish. Against gargantuan monsters, evasion and repositioning matter as much as damage output. That design choice pushes the game closer to a platforming-first Metroidvania than a straight action title.

### The Scanner Bot Changes How You Read a Room

Your scanner bot, paired with X-ray vision, lets you inspect creatures and terrain more closely. It reveals hidden paths and secret areas that are invisible to the naked eye, and it helps you map where you have been. In practice, this turns observation into a mechanic: rooms are puzzles to be *read* before they are solved.

### Protoplasm and the Metamorphosis Loop

Killing hostile organisms lets you absorb Protoplasm, described as lumps of energy-packed biomass. You then consume it inside organic chambers to trigger metamorphosis, which upgrades your abilities and weapons. It is a tidy loop that ties combat directly to progression instead of routing everything through a shop.

## Practical Tips for Your First Hours on Protea VI

Community reports from early players consistently point in the same direction: slow down before you speed up. Moadra rewards reconnaissance. Here are habits worth building immediately.

- **Scan before you commit.** The scanner bot is not a collectible gimmick. Use X-ray vision at the entrance of every new room and note what it exposes.
- **Treat sprint as a dodge tool.** Movement abilities double as survival tools. Practicing roll and wall-jump chains early pays off when a room demands precision.
- **Bank Protoplasm deliberately.** Since metamorphosis happens in organic chambers, plan your upgrades rather than absorbing biomass reflexively.
- **Map your backtracking.** Hidden paths revealed by the scanner often connect areas you have already cleared. Note shortcuts so you are not re-running long corridors.
- **Respect the freezing summits.** Vertical zones punish sloppy timing. Clear them when you are fresh, not at the end of a session.

| Priority | Action | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Learn the movement set | Traversal is survival, not decoration |
| 2 | Scan every new area | Hidden paths and secrets are scanner-gated |
| 3 | Plan metamorphosis upgrades | Protoplasm is finite early on |
| 4 | Note shortcuts and loops | Reduces backtracking friction |
| 5 | Test weapons on low-level threats | Builds reflexes before big encounters |
