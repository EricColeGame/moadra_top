## What You Can Actually Try in the Demo

Gloomsoft hasn't published a scene-by-scene breakdown of demo contents, so treat any exact list you see elsewhere with suspicion. What the store page does confirm is the system set the game is built around — and those are the systems the demo build carries.

- **Movement kit.** Roll, dodge, leap, wall jump, and sprint, all tuned for evading organisms and environmental hazards rather than tanking them.
- **Combat and weapon variety.** An arsenal that scales from small threats to bosses that force you to use every tool you've unlocked.
- **The scanner bot.** X-ray vision reveals creatures' details and exposes hidden paths and secrets invisible to the naked eye, while mapping where you've been.
- **Protoplasm and metamorphosis.** Blow up hostile organisms, absorb the biomass they drop, and spend it in organic chambers to evolve abilities and weapons.
- **Nonlinear exploration.** Protea VI is a large interconnected world with optional routes, so even a short demo can branch in several directions.

One caveat worth stating plainly: demo scope is a studio decision, and Gloomsoft can trim or expand it at any time. Check the store page before you plan an evening around it.
